    How to play

    Type a real word and press Enter. A teal tile is the right letter in the right spot, an amber tile is in the word somewhere else, and a grey tile is not in the word. A repeated letter is only marked as many times as the answer has it.

    4 and 5-letter words get 6 tries; 6 and 7-letter words get 7. Everyone gets the same daily word for their date. Fewest guesses wins the leaderboard, and time breaks ties.
